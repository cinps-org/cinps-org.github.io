Getting started

  1. Add courses, subjects and chapters.
  2. Add MCQs and mark the correct option.
  3. Create an examination from selected chapter questions.
  4. After the student portal is connected, review submissions and download results as CSV.

Correct answers stay in the admin-only question bank. Do not copy answer keys into student-visible exam documents.
